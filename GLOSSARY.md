# Product selection

Domain terms for evidence-based discovery and evaluation of physical product forms. These definitions preserve the current product requirements; they do not resolve pending scoring or market-reference choices.

## Language

**Discovery signal (发现线索)**:
A sourced market observation, product record or recurring use need that gives a reason to investigate a direction. It is neither proof of an attractive opportunity nor a keyword invented without supporting evidence.
_Avoid_: Proven opportunity, arbitrary seed word

**Research direction (研究方向)**:
A demand, use or market area being investigated for potential product forms. It may yield several materially different candidates.
_Avoid_: Candidate when no product form has been defined

**Normal-from-zero newcomer (正常从零起步新品)**:
A genuinely recent launch whose observed start is suitable as a reference without an inherited older review base. A listing with old reviews clearly predating its purported recent launch falls outside this reference, regardless of the unestablished mechanism behind that history.
_Avoid_: Source newcomer label alone as proof of a fresh start, a new child as proof of a new family

**Candidate product form (候选款式)**:
A product possibility described by its use, material, structure, dimensions, functionality and bundle. Color-only variants belong together, while material differences in the offer may require separate evaluation.
_Avoid_: ASIN as the sole definition of a candidate

**Comparable niche (可比细分市场)**:
The scoped population of genuinely substitutable offers relevant to a candidate's use and specifications. Different materials can belong to the same competitive market when buyers actually compare them for the same need and key requirements are met; each distinct candidate form retains its own costs and score. Material-driven differences in required load capacity, durability or explicit buyer material requirements can prevent that substitution. A broad BSR node is separate context; operational membership verification, observation windows and score mappings still need definition and validation.
_Avoid_: Broad category as a synonym for the direct comparison population

**Head-group sales concentration (头部销量集中度)**:
The share of a declared sample's estimated unit sales attributed to its largest specified number of product, brand or seller groups during one source-defined period. The sample-selection rule, head count and grouping dimension are part of its meaning; the sample need not be a current BSR list.
_Avoid_: Share of entity counts, share of sales revenue, native top-ten share as a share-square index

**Selection evidence (选品证据)**:
An inspectable observation or source material relevant to a research question, with its origin, time and applicable scope. An interpretation based on it is a judgment rather than another measured observation.
_Avoid_: AI assertion as a synonym for observed evidence

**Main purchase entry (主购买入口)**:
A relevant search query through which buyers are meaningfully likely to encounter offers serving the candidate's intended use and specifications, supported by available market or competitor evidence. A longtail query can be a main entry; word length or a cheap suggested bid alone does not determine its role.
_Avoid_: Every returned keyword as an equally important entry, core/longtail as fixed score budgets

**Native suggested bid (原生建议竞价)**:
A provider-returned suggested bidding amount for a keyword under a stated marketplace, currency, match type and observation basis. Its suggested range is separate from the point, and neither is an observed CPC for the prospective product.
_Avoid_: Actual CPC, inferred native midpoint, predicted customer-acquisition cost

**Entry-weighted bid reference (主入口竞价参考值)**:
A project-derived comparison amount combining supported native suggested points according to declared research importance across selected main purchase entries. It remains distinct from any provider-native point, actual paid cost or proposed account bidding instruction.
_Avoid_: Provider-native aggregate, future ad-budget split, break-even CPC

**Observed seller account (可观察卖家账号)**:
A source-reported selling account in a specified marketplace, identified preferentially by seller ID with its displayed name retained separately. It is distinct from a brand and does not establish hidden corporate control across stores.
_Avoid_: Brand owner, proven independent company, same display name as proof of the same account

**Seller dispersion (卖家分散程度 / 卖家集中度的反向偏好)**:
The distribution of the comparable market's supported observations among observed seller accounts, with lower concentration preferred. The source's seller-attribution meaning remains explicit; this is not a claim to know every seller's actual orders or ultimate company ownership.
_Avoid_: Hidden-company concentration, full-store coverage inferred from a returned page

**Niche purchase-demand scale (细分市场购买需求规模)**:
The supported estimated sales units in a declared, deduplicated comparable niche over a stated period. It describes observed buying activity, not search counts, revenue or the candidate's obtainable sales.
_Avoid_: Broad-category volume, summed keyword searches, own-sales forecast

**Supported demand opportunity (有依据的需求机会)**:
Evidence that a comparable niche has durable recurring demand, sustained growth or an underserved buying need. Demand-side support differs from the candidate's ability to satisfy that need.
_Avoid_: Popularity alone, low competition alone, an unverified improvement idea

**Purchase-entry match (购买入口匹配)**:
The fit between evidenced main buying intents and the candidate's intended use and consequential specifications. It describes what buyers are seeking, not proof that the candidate performs as claimed.
_Avoid_: Keyword-count score, cheap-bid opportunity, verified product quality

**Existing-solution fit (现成方案满足需求)**:
The supported functional suitability of a concrete candidate form for its intended use and requirements. It concerns the product's evidenced capabilities rather than the wording of its buying entries or an imagined future repair.
_Avoid_: Search-intent match, verified future quality

**Feasible light differentiation (可行轻改进)**:
A supported small change to an existing form that offers a practical benefit for an identified use problem, with an initial feasibility basis. It is optional and does not imply confirmed manufacturing, rights clearance or actual tested performance.
_Avoid_: Novelty for its own sake, an unsupported improvement idea

**Preliminary specific-form rights check (具体款式权利初查)**:
A bounded, recorded review of relevant brand/content, appearance and functional-rights evidence for a concrete form and intended market. Its scope, unresolved clues and limitations remain visible; a favorable preliminary result does not establish exhaustive clearance.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed non-infringement, generic appearance as clearance, suspected conflict as a numerical probability

**Known entry burden (已知准入负担)**:
The supported practical work required to meet applicable product and marketplace conditions for a concrete form, from ordinary documentation to additional testing or substantial redesign. Unknown applicability is a distinct evidence state; a supported route is not proof that a future supplier shipment already complies.
_Avoid_: Unverified supplier claim, category-label exclusion, duplicate monetary deduction

**Applicable public requirements (准入要求)**:
The legal and marketplace conditions applicable to selling a particular product in its target market, including relevant safety testing, documentation and labeling. Their practical clarity and difficulty differ from specific-form intellectual-property conflicts and commercial profitability.
_Avoid_: Patent clearance, a universal certificate required for every product

**Cross-source observed range (跨来源观测范围)**:
The source-labeled spread of available readings for the same metric meaning across comparable product scopes and periods. It summarizes provider observations while preserving their original values and dates; it is not a statistical confidence interval, a merged count or a guarantee about the true population.
_Avoid_: True keyword-count bounds, consensus midpoint, an exposure percentage derived from word counts

**SIF traffic share (SIF 流量占比)**:
SIF's source-defined modeled exposure share for a specified product, period and denominator. The interface uses a traffic label; this metric does not report actual clicks, sessions, orders or raw measured impression totals. A whole-listing share and a within-keyword or within-channel share have different denominators.
_Avoid_: Actual organic order share, every provider's definition of traffic

**Rating count (评分数量 / 来源标注的评分人数)**:
The source-reported accumulated rating count for a specified product scope and timestamp. Verify the field meaning before use; do not assume it is a count of distinct buyers or written reviews. A current value cannot silently stand in for the count at an earlier sales or BSR event.
_Avoid_: Star rating, written review count

**Written review count (文字评论条数)**:
The source-reported count of written reviews for an identified product scope and time, using the provider's verified definition. It is distinct from the number of star ratings, the displayed star value and the share of complaints.
_Avoid_: Rating count

**Comparable-newcomer rating-count median (可比新品评价数中位数)**:
The median accumulated rating count of the eligible comparable-newcomer reference cohort at aligned observation points, adopted for trial as the reference for relative rating accumulation. It describes the cohort's middle rating-count level, not a rating requirement for sales success or a substitute for missing launch history.
_Avoid_: Whole-category review average, required reviews to launch, provider-native reference field

**Star rating (星级)**:
The displayed overall star value on the source's stated scale and product scope. It describes the rating value, not how many ratings or written reviews exist.
_Avoid_: Rating count, low-rating-count opportunity

**Comparable-niche sample mean star rating (同细分市场样本平均星级)**:
The equal-weight arithmetic mean of supported displayed star values across deduplicated rating units in the observed comparable-niche sample, including established products and newcomers. Its date and valid coverage identify the reference; it is not a pooled customer-rating average, a selection cutoff or a prediction of the owner's future star rating.
_Avoid_: Whole-market census, newcomer rating-count median, missing stars as zero

**Sales progress with few accumulated ratings (少评价积累下的销售进展)**:
Sales in units considered jointly with the accumulated rating count at the relevant time, for comparable product forms, launch stages and observation windows. A minority of supported normal-start examples can provide a reference without implying easy entry for most newcomers; the observed comparison set, counterexamples and unknown histories remain part of that judgment.
_Avoid_: Low-star sales, sales divided by reviews, an approved numeric threshold

**Current-observation few-rating sales reference (当前少评价销售参照)**:
A separately labeled comparison of supported current rating counts and sales from a consistent observation period among comparable-stage newcomers in the accepted BSR reference scope. It describes present performance, not the rating count or sales conditions at first entry, and is kept separate from event-time reference observations. It may serve as a common current comparison basis without erasing available historical evidence.
_Avoid_: Reconstructed first-entry history, current count substituted for past count

**Observed comparable-newcomer reference cohort (已观察到的可比新品参照池)**:
The observed newcomers that meet the declared substitutable-niche, relevant BSR observation, launch-stage and evidence-basis conditions for a particular comparison. Membership, supported values, unresolved observations and coverage are visible; this is not a claim to have found the entire market. New-Releases-only products are supporting evidence until they also meet the primary reference conditions.
_Avoid_: A fixed six-product sample, only the best-performing examples, whole-category membership

**Selected newcomer exemplars (选定新品案例)**:
The qualifying examples selected from an observed reference cohort to summarize a supported opportunity. They remain distinct from the larger cohort that supplies the market references and contrary evidence; the current two-example aggregation does not redefine market size.
_Avoid_: The full niche, a census, a success-probability sample

**Single-case selection evidence (单例选品线索)**:
Supported evidence from one eligible observed launch, which can retain its own justified example score and prompt further investigation. It does not by itself establish a complete direction-level score, typical newcomer performance or a market success rate; other observed outcomes and uncertainty remain relevant.
_Avoid_: A second independent example, complete market score, candidate failure

**Newcomer sales-unit share (新品销量件数占比)**:
The estimated units attributed to source-defined eligible newcomers divided by estimated units for the same declared sample and sales period, with consistent family scope. The native market sample used for the count/sales shares is distinct from the genuinely comparable normal-start cohort used for few-rating sales evidence. Q31 uses the latest 30 days as the primary sales window and three months as the newcomer age definition. Its trial mapping and selected population are recorded in the reference profile; actual coverage and calibration remain to verify.
_Avoid_: Revenue share, lifetime sales share, newcomer success probability

**Selection card (选品卡)**:
A reviewable account of a candidate, its discovery basis, supporting evidence, economics, scoring results or explicit unknowns, and reasons for a next step.
_Avoid_: Procurement instruction

**Research priority (研究优先级)**:
The relative need to investigate a direction or fill an evidence gap next. It is separate from the assessed attractiveness of a candidate product form.
_Avoid_: Opportunity score

**Opportunity score (选品综合分)**:
An assessment of a candidate against the agreed weighted business criteria, with its evidence and uncertainty visible. Evidence completeness alone does not define this score or commercial success.
_Avoid_: Evidence-completeness score, procurement approval

**Needs evidence (待补证)**:
An evaluation condition in which missing, conflicting or insufficiently comparable evidence prevents a supported conclusion. It is not a numerical score or proof of unattractiveness.
_Avoid_: Zero score, failed product

**Conditional score range (条件性可能得分范围)**:
The bounds of a possible item score under stated valid sample and known-input assumptions when a required input is missing. It accompanies an unscored result and is neither an observed score nor a statistical confidence interval.
_Avoid_: Earned lower-bound points, midpoint score, cross-source observed range

**Unscored true-zero reference (真实零基准未评分)**:
The trial evaluation state in which a verified comparable-newcomer rating-count median is zero and the relative-rating component has no numerical score. The underlying observations remain valid; independently supported points are a partial result, not a complete item score.
_Avoid_: Missing observations, zero score, full score

**Recorded selection decision (已记录选品判断)**:
The owner's stated disposition and rationale for a candidate at a particular stage, retained with its evidence context. Continuing research or retaining a candidate does not itself authorize spending or establish commercial success.
_Avoid_: Purchase approval, validated profitability
