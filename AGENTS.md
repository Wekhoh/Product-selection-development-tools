## Independence and privacy

Runtime, imports, configuration, tests, and project recovery must work from this repository without another operations repository or its services. Historical references are evidence only. Reuse old implementations only after checking their current assumptions, dependencies, license, and fit to the agreed design.

Keep credentials, original conversations, raw provider payloads, genuine candidate/business data, supplier quotations, spreadsheets, and live request/budget ledgers outside tracked files. `.local/`, `data/`, and `outputs/` are ignored local locations; ignore rules are not a substitute for staged-file review.

Before activating a migrated or newly wired paid integration, reconcile existing in-flight/unknown requests and spending state, including strictly read-only reconciliation against the canonical legacy `budget.sqlite3` ledger. Paid OpenAI HTTP paths remain disabled by default (`enabled: false`) in offline and CI runs; monthly subscription API credits (`$200/month` steady-state ceiling, expiring at cycle end without rollover) require platform `Auto-recharge OFF` plus a hard monthly limit, conservative integer-cent SQLite reservation before dispatch, `max_retries = 0` on billable `POST` requests, and cross-month `pending_recovery` submission fencing on ambiguous timeouts/5xx. A new folder or checkout does not reset the budget or authorize another submission. Migration and adapter wiring do not enable scans, schedules, supplier messages, procurement, advertising, or store writes.

Prefer offline validation and standard hosted CI runners. Do not trigger paid provider calls from CI. Use PowerShell 7 on Windows, Node.js 24 ESM for core runtime and tests, and UTF-8 Python execution for auxiliary scripts. Keep project instructions in English and preserve source identifiers and original evidence where needed.
