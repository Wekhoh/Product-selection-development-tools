# US FBA fee basis for initial product economics

Checked **2026-11-14**. Scope: Amazon.com marketplace FBA and the fees that can materially affect this project's per-saleable-unit estimate. This is a dated rules review, not an account audit, a universal rate configuration or a completed profitability assessment. Original candidate inputs and arithmetic readbacks remain private.

## Evidence and effective dates

Amazon's public pricing page was read directly. Most Seller Central help URLs returned navigation/JavaScript shells; their substantive rules and tables were inspected through indexed Amazon-owned text. Named Amazon employee replies corroborated the original fulfillment-fee treatment. This is not authenticated fee-report verification or a claim that all help pages were read directly in full. Exa fetches independently confirmed the shell limitation; unrelated merchant-fulfilled, MCF, Export and Remote Fulfillment policies were excluded from the US marketplace calculation.

Several current help pages already display 2027 announcements alongside current tables. Apply each table to its stated event date: fulfillment shipment, inbound-plan creation, storage month or returned shipment cohort. Do not use publication/crawl date alone. In particular, the current holiday fulfillment window is October 15, 2026 through January 14, 2027, while storage peak months are October through December. These calendars differ. [Fulfillment policy](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/GPDC3KPYAGDTVDJP?ld=SCFBAStriplogin), [monthly storage](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G3EDYEF6KUCFQTNM).

## Classify the fee before applying its rate

Amazon fee categories and customer-facing browse/BSR categories are separate. An ASIN has one fee category even when it appears in multiple browse categories. Amazon recommends the Fee Preview report for an existing ASIN's actual assignment. Where unavailable for a research candidate, record a supported guideline-based classification as an estimate, not an account observation. A common referral percentage alone does not identify the category. The current guideline lists travel/neck pillows and cabinet pull-out organizers under Home and Kitchen; this is a product-type example, not evidence about every actual ASIN. Preserve the research-market node separately. [Fee category guidelines](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/GFD6HLGAEZC9VBGJ?locale=enUS).

Category-average physical returns, actual refunded revenue, and the fee category's excess-return threshold are three different inputs. A smaller BSR niche does not automatically have its own billing threshold. Record source, category, units, period and whether the classification is observed or inferred before joining these inputs.

Amazon's excess-return cohort comprises a shipment month's units physically returned in that month and the next two calendar months; billing occurs in the third subsequent month. Third-party recent-return observations do not reproduce this billing cohort exactly. Thresholds can be reviewed quarterly. Do not transplant a historical exemption, a buyer-paid return option or a seller-fulfilled label charge into this calculation. No independent universal second outbound-equivalent fee was established for ordinary US FBA returns; apply the supported program-specific return rules rather than inventing one. [Returns processing](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/GZGEQLTM3RZXUV6T?locale=en-), [FBA fee/service overview](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/201074400?locale=en_US).

### Weight and season reconciliation

For current Large Standard, the complete packaged saleable unit must fit the applicable 18 x 14 x 8 inch and 20 lb limits. Charge weight uses the greater of unit weight and dimensional weight; dimensional pounds use cubic inches divided by 139. The freight carrier's divisor and carton rules remain separate. Amazon measurements ultimately govern billed charges. [Size tiers](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/GG5KW835AHDJCH8W), [dimensional weight](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G53Z9EKF8VVZVH29?locale=es-ES).

The 2026 non-apparel Large Standard 3–20 lb table uses 4-ounce increments above the first 3 lb, rounded up by interval. For prices above $50, the peak base is `$7.77 + $0.08 * ceil((weight_lb - 3) / 0.25)`, before the 3.5% surcharge. Exactly $50 remains in the middle price band. The historical 2026 non-peak base for that row is $7.23; it is not a promise about a future non-peak shipment. Recalculate fee tiers when a proposed selling-price range crosses a band. [2026 fulfillment table](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/GABBX6GZPA8MSZGW?locale=en_us).

Do not label a calculation a coherent non-peak scenario when only the storage selector changed but fulfillment still uses the current holiday rate. Record both periods, or construct matched period scenarios. Rounding in third-party displayed package dimensions can explain cent-level differences; do not rewrite a calculator observation to fit a rounded proxy without evidence.

## Formula and accounting checks

For a complete single saleable unit, a labeled initial full-refund proxy `r` gives:

1. `net_revenue = selling_price * (1 - r)`.
2. With a single-unit fully refunded non-media order line, `net_referral = gross_referral * (1 - r) + r * min(5, 0.20 * gross_referral)`. Compute `gross_referral` from the category's actual tier/minimum rule first.
3. Charge procurement, packaging, inbound freight and original FBA fulfillment once per original shipment. Removing refunded sales does not justify charging those goods again as a second loss. No speculative recovered-inventory value offsets them in the current initial scope.
4. `contribution = net_revenue - included_net_unit_costs`; `margin = contribution / net_revenue`.
5. Collection applies to the estimated Amazon payout after included Amazon cash deductions, not selling price, product profit or an amount already reduced by off-Amazon procurement/freight. A noncash inventory credit is not a payout deduction.
6. The optional mature advertising illustration subtracts 15% of reference selling price from contribution and retains the refund-net denominator. It is not a subtraction of 15 margin percentage points or a forecast of launch economics.

Items 1 and 3–6 are the owner's accounting conventions, not claims that Amazon defines the project's margin. Category returns are only a proxy for refund incidence; realized amounts replace it later. The ledger explicitly separates deferred disposition/recovery from missing necessary costs. The existing 30% boundary applies to the supported stated estimate; it is not proof of realized profitability or permission to invent other missing inputs.
