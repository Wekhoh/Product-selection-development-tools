### Why the 2.5-point item is the immediate simplification candidate

For a synthetic rack needing manageable assembly, the adopted routine-complexity anchor gives 0.5/1. Deducting more under entry barriers solely because of that same assembly work would count the same operating burden again. No such second deduction is currently implemented or approved.

A specific commercial access constraint could in principle convey additional information. The review did not establish a relevant, repeatably observable case for this first version. Do not invent a complicated new investigation merely to fill the reserved 2.5 points.

**Concrete lead proposal, not adopted:** retain the competition group's 10-point budget but use product dispersion **2**, brand dispersion **2**, and observable seller dispersion **6**, removing the standalone entry-barrier item. This preserves the existing concentration weights' 1:1:3 relationship and leaves all other group budgets unchanged. It deliberately increases the concentration block from 7.5 to 10 points; it is not a neutral rename or a normalization around missing evidence. The owner must decide whether that stronger concentration emphasis is appropriate. Applicable requirements and routine-work evidence stay in their existing items; other material access observations remain visible context. No concentration mapping is approved by this allocation proposal. These numbers belong to the competition group and do not revive any historical Q11 allocation.

### Why three concentration views are not automatically redundant

Ten products, ten brands and ten seller accounts can have identical share distributions. Under the unapproved formula, ten equal shares would yield 7.5 combined points; an 80% leader with nine equally sharing 20% would yield approximately 2.963. This is one distribution receiving the block's combined preference weight, not three independent pieces of evidence.

The groupings can also differ: ten equally selling products under one brand and seller, ten brands under one seller, and ten brands under ten sellers describe different structures. Therefore the conditional equality does not justify silently deleting the three approved views. The formula itself remains unapproved; none of these synthetic points belongs to a real candidate.
