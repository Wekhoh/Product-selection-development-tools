# Niche demand: 16-point trial rules

Updated: 2026-11-13. **The owner accepts the complete 4/6/6 demand proposal for trial**, following the earlier weight-only acceptance in reference-profile revision 37. Revision 38 records all three mappings below: the scale curve, opportunity rubric and purchase-entry match rubric. The independent initial-goods 6-point score remains retired. Actual acquisition coverage, judgment consistency and cross-category calibration are unverified. All example products, quantities and evidence are synthetic. This acceptance does not authorize live retrieval or activate production code.

The three questions are: how much observable buying activity exists, what supports a durable demand opportunity, and whether the main purchase intents actually match this product form. Keep them within the existing three items rather than adding submetrics and weights. A small stable niche can score well overall; demand points are not a success probability or a whole-product recommendation.

## Accepted trial scale mapping: 4 points

Use supported recent-30-day estimated sales units for a declared genuinely comparable niche population, not sales revenue, an arbitrary pair of ASINs or the sum of keyword search volumes. Search trends corroborate demand and are not purchases. The practical population and source coverage must be validated in the later trial; do not promise an exhaustive market census.

| Supported units in the declared comparable population | Trial points |
| ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 0 |
| 100 | 1 |
| 500 | 2 |
| 2,000 | 3 |
| 5,000 or more | 4 |

Interpolate linearly between adjacent anchors, cap at 4, and retain the raw units above the cap. These are deliberately provisional calibration marks, not observed industry boundaries, minimum viable markets or exclusion rules. Diminishing increments keep a large market from overwhelming a smaller niche solely through size. Full points mean reaching this trial scale cap, not low competition or an obtainable share of sales.

Use one documented scope and period with no repeated parent-family totals or overlapping variant observations. A broad category total, mixed-spec family total or unexamined truncated page is not a supported niche total. If coverage, scope, period or sales is materially unresolved, retain the available data and pending score; do not convert missing data to zero, extrapolate a short observation to a month or silently use a midpoint. Estimates are acceptable when their basis is adequate; backend precision is not required. Existing population evidence may be reused after checking its fit; the proposal does not require a new exhaustive investigation for every candidate.

## Accepted trial opportunity rubric: 6 points

Use four coarse anchors. Evaluate the strongest supported route—stable demand, sustained growth, or a demand-side supply gap—rather than adding bonuses for each route. Stable demand can earn the full 6 without growth or a new product invention. An unmet need is demand evidence; our ability to solve it belongs to the separate need-fit/light-improvement group.

| Points | Trial meaning |
| ---: | --- |
| 0 | Adequate observed evidence supports none of the three routes; the proposed opportunity is contradicted, rather than merely unsearched. |
| 2 | A real but weak opportunity signal remains after inspecting relevant evidence, such as a measured short spike that has not persisted in the observed follow-up. |
| 4 | One route is supported, but observed counterevidence materially weakens its durability or extent; for example a sustained improvement with repeated reversals. |
| 6 | At least one route is well supported under the applicable observed context: durable recurring demand, sustained non-artifactual growth, or repeated purchase-intent evidence of an underserved need. No known material counterevidence defeats that interpretation. |

Do not award points merely because the market is large, multiple providers repeat one signal, newcomer share is high, concentration is low or somebody posts an improvement idea. Those are separate measures or unverified assertions. Inspect relevant low-base, seasonal/event and incomplete-period explanations using available history. Missing required history is an evidence gap, not an automatic 0 or 2; avoid claiming causal proof or requiring every imaginable alternative to be disproved. The first trial must check whether these coarse judgments are consistent and economical to produce.

## Accepted trial purchase-entry match rubric: 6 points

Judge the relevant main buying intents against the candidate's use, function and consequential specification/bundle. Keyword wording alone is insufficient; inspect available search/product/traffic evidence. Broad words can still match well when their actual buying intent matches. Exact wording and many low-volume longtails do not automatically earn a higher score.

| Points | Trial meaning |
| ---: | --- |
| 0 | Evidence shows that the main purported buying entries seek a materially different use or form. |
| 2 | Only a limited part of the observed main intent fits; the major entries mix substantially different buyer needs. |
| 4 | Main uses generally fit, but a material part of those entries seeks a different size, configuration or other consequential feature. |
| 6 | The evidenced main buying intents match the candidate's core use and relevant constraints; minor wording differences do not matter. |

Do not reward raw keyword count, natural visibility, cheap bids or large volume again in this item. Preserve relevant core/longtail explanations; many tiny matching entries must not conceal mismatch in the main entries. Suggested-bid pressure remains its separate 15-point group. Missing entry evidence stays pending. Whether a proposed form can actually deliver its claimed performance remains a separate need-fit and later verification question.

## Execution and missing evidence

Codex identifies the comparable scope, evaluates the opportunity and entry evidence, and attaches short source-grounded reasons to each selected qualitative anchor. Deterministic code validates identifiers, scope/window fields and duplicates where possible, performs the adopted arithmetic, and retains rule/evidence versions. Ambiguous substitutability remains a judgment, not an exact-ID deduplication rule. No new model service or generic scoring framework is required by these trial rules.

If one item is unavailable, show the supported items and the missing item explicitly. For example, independently supported opportunity and entry values of 6 each with missing scale mean a supported 12 points plus an unscored 4-point item, not an awarded 12/16 final score or a normalized 16/16. Overall missingness and ordering follow the adopted revision-50 shared rules in DECISIONS and the reference profile; do not invent certainty here.

After this whole-group acceptance, validate actual source fields and varied real cases in a separately authorized bounded trial. Check small stable niches, genuine growth, misleading spikes, weak entry matches and missing coverage; inspect missed opportunities and inflated scores. Revise anchors from those results rather than defending the provisional numbers as settled market facts. There is no requirement to interview every edge case before trying the workflow.
