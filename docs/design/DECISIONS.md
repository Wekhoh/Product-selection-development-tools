# Design decisions and open questions

Status: preserved design direction, not a completed scoring implementation. Updated 2026-11-11.

The numerical trial budgets and conditions have one machine-readable source: [reference-profile.draft.json](reference-profile.draft.json). This document preserves the reasons, exceptions, and question lineage that numbers alone cannot represent.

## Confirmed direction

- **Research output (Q1/Q2/Q20):** broad brief cards, deeper work on priority candidates, and explainable comparison. Completeness of evidence is not product attractiveness. Display pagination is not a discovery, testing, or procurement cap.
- **Product and supply stage (Q4/Q8):** initially favor existing products and small orders; lightweight differentiation may be useful but is not mandatory. Private tooling generally follows market validation, not a permanent prohibition. Group related directions; evaluate materially different forms separately. Color-only variants do not create multiple opportunities.
- **Economics (Q3/Q7/Q9/Q18/Q21):** use a coherent same-specification reference transaction price and full applicable recurring/expected costs. Separate upfront sampling, creative work, and program costs from unit margin. The actual collection provider fee, its basis, and exchange settlement must be verified; do not stack historical 0.6%/0.7% defaults. Initial goods-value estimates are not actual cash-flow or payback forecasts. Analysis quantities are not purchase authorization.
- **Cost uncertainty (Q18):** first improve comparable procurement, packaging, freight and fee evidence. If the remaining credible margin range crosses the configured floor, retain a needs-evidence result. Missing costs are not zero.
- **Normal starts (Q5/Q11/Q13):** inspect both newcomer counts and comparable sales shares, with listing age, old reviews, parent/child changes, related operators, and advertising/natural-visibility trajectories. Apparent anomalies need investigation, not accusations. Preserve failed/exited/immature observations where available.
- **Bid evidence (Q12/Q14/Q23/Q30):** absolute suggested-bid pressure is primary, niche-relative context secondary. Use the native point for the main score and retain lower/upper-bound score scenarios separately. Equal native points under the same mapping have equal main scores; a wider range does not automatically lose points. Do not invent a native midpoint or treat suggested bids as actual CPC. Aggregate deduplicated relevant purchase entries so major buying entries carry the main pressure. Preserve core/longtail views and cheaper smaller-entry opportunities separately; many cheap small terms must not dilute the main pressure. Research importance is not future ad allocation, and exact estimation/aggregation remains unresolved.
- **Multiple candidates (Q17):** several candidates may continue research and testing; do not force a single winner. Real tests determine later capital concentration.
- **Rights (Q19/Q32):** confirmed conflict excludes the particular specification and jurisdiction being evaluated. Investigate other forms independently. Suspected, unsearched, or one expired patent is not comprehensive clearance; cosmetic changes alone do not prove avoidance.
- **Demand and concentration (Q22/Q24):** use genuine purchase intent and the directly comparable niche. Preserve product, brand and evidenced operator concentration separately; suspected common ownership is not proven ownership. Broad BSR statistics remain context.
- **Risk and timing (Q26/Q27):** lower return risk is a preference without a new automatic return-rate cutoff. Count monetary losses once in economics. Defer strongly holiday/date-dependent specific forms in the current reference profile; this is not all seasonal products or a permanent category ban. Storage and transit expectations require relevant conditions and evidence. Material or powered-product labels alone are not exclusions; applicable safety and market requirements still need investigation.
- **Phase order:** public or sourced estimated evidence can support an initial card. Supplier contact, own samples, actual lead time, quality control and real CPC/CVR are later evidence. Before a 1688 inquiry, the owner must review the exact supplier targets and complete message and explicitly authorize sending to those targets; this is not blanket contact permission or permission for other channels. A stage transition is not sending authorization; a score never authorizes procurement or store writes.

## Q11 to Q31: the unresolved substance

Q11 proposed an abstract reproducibility allocation of 2/2/6. That proposal was not adopted. The owner instead asked how normal launches, inherited reviews, variations, and traffic development support the conclusion.

Q31 continues that question: what sales progress is meaningful at a given rating accumulation, which observation windows and comparable samples apply, and how broad BSR top-100 evidence relates to the direct niche. Do not restart from Q11's withdrawn allocation or treat Q31 as already resolved.

Current discussion references include a specified BSR node and the sales/rating distribution of comparable products. Preserve newcomer entry counts, contemporaneous rating accumulation, and entry/retention/exit paths where observable, separately from comparable niche sales. A top-100 snapshot cannot establish all launches, timing, success rates or exact sales thresholds. Median sales and a lower review-count quartile are proposed calibration aids, not approved rules. Two arbitrary ASINs cannot define a market score. Synthetic quantities such as 80 units or 40 reviews are examples, not thresholds.

## Still unresolved

- Q31's definitions, comparison populations, observation windows, internal weights, joint mapping and multi-comparator aggregation.
- Q32's internal rights/public-requirement allocation.
- All numeric mapping curves, calibration parameters and qualitative scoring anchors.
- Missingness/interval ranking, denominator handling and duplicate-signal contributions.
- A comparable initial-goods quantity and price-tier basis.
- Complete product architecture and an owner-reviewed real-card acceptance flow.
