# Independent product-selection tool: requirements candidate

Status: migration design material, revision 2 after independent semantic review. This document preserves a research direction; it is not a finished implementation or an approval to activate live research.

## Outcome

Discover multiple promising physical product forms from real demand evidence, explain their economics and entry difficulty, and produce inspectable selection cards for a human decision maker. Support configurable preferences and comparison across genuinely substitutable niches. The reference profile is one trial profile, not a universal commercial standard.

The project must be independent: its checkout, imports, configuration, tests, data paths and recovery instructions must not require a former store-operations repository. Do not migrate autonomous store control, advertising execution, unrelated operating history or old schedulers as dependencies.

## Discovery and analysis

Start with measured keyword and market data rather than a fixed list of imagined words. Investigate stable demand, growth and supported supply gaps. Communities and other marketplaces may discover new directions or corroborate pain points; popularity alone does not prove purchase demand or low competition.

Define the use, material, structure, dimensions, functionality and bundle before building a comparable sample. Preserve broad BSR context separately from same-niche measures. Include failed and exited newcomers where coverage supports it. An apparent review jump, old review or parent change needs investigation; it is not automatically proof of manipulation. Distinguish Vine, verified-purchase and other review evidence where available; Vine alone is not abnormal. Ad-heavy visibility alone does not prove a product cannot profit, and modeled natural visibility is not natural orders.

Preserve BSR top-100 newcomer entry counts, contemporaneous rating accumulation and entry/retention/exit paths where observable, separately from comparable niche sales evidence. A reference for low-rating sales performance is still unresolved; arbitrary pairs of products do not settle a market-level judgment.

Use full applicable unit economics, matched packed dimensions/weight and dated fees. Keep pre-ad product margin separate from an explicitly labeled mature-stage advertising scenario. Suggested bids are references, not measured CPC; a new product's CVR cannot be invented to calculate expected CPA.

Suggested-bid aggregation follows major buying-entry importance, while core/longtail views and cheaper smaller-entry opportunities remain visible. Do not dilute the main pressure by averaging many cheap small terms. Research weights do not represent future ad allocation. Native point values drive the main score; bounds remain separate scenarios, without an automatic wide-range penalty or invented native midpoint. Exact selection, weights and mappings remain to be calibrated.

## Scoring status

The companion JSON preserves nine confirmed trial group budgets totaling 100 and confirmed internal allocations. A null item weight or mapping is an unresolved design choice, not zero. The profile is deliberately ineligible for production.

Higher margin and lower acquisition pressure should help the score. Confirmed specification-level rights conflicts and the configured margin condition remain distinct screening results. Uncertainty crossing a condition stays pending. Avoid fixed review-count, CPC and broad-category newcomer cutoffs.

The following remain unsettled: normal-newcomer performance definitions and references, remaining internal weights, numerical mappings and calibration, evidence/missingness aggregation, and the complete implementation and real-card acceptance. Do not restore superseded historical formulas because they are executable.

## Cards and human decisions

Provide brief cards broadly and deepen priority candidates. Show sources, windows, assumptions, per-item points or unknowns, exclusions and missing evidence. Group related forms and avoid inflating opportunity counts with color-only changes. Pagination limits display, not discovery.

Initial research uses public product and supply evidence without requiring supplier contact. Prefer existing designs suitable for small-batch investigation; optional light changes need feasibility evidence. Private molds are usually a later consideration after market and profit validation, not permanently forbidden. Do not presume an industry pain point is solvable by this team.

Supplier inquiry, actual procurement, sampling, measured advertising and operational decisions are separate later stages. Before any 1688 inquiry, the owner must review the specific supplier targets and complete message and explicitly authorize sending it. This does not grant blanket contact permission or extend to other channels. Avoid claiming legal clearance or profitability from a structural validator or synthetic demonstration.

## Public and private boundary

Public deliverables may contain sanitized methods, schemas, configurable reference settings and synthetic fixtures after review. Keep credentials, raw conversations, vendor responses, actual store data, supplier quotations, private spreadsheets, live ledgers and machine paths local and outside Git. Do not copy private Git history.

Preserve local evidence and provenance through a private handoff index. A migration must not reset spending or unresolved-request state, silently retry external requests, or activate historical schedules. Reconcile those concerns separately before any later live activation.

## Migration acceptance

- Fresh isolated checkout and offline tests work without the former repository, its environment, local caches or absolute paths.
- Imported modules and fixtures have a concrete use, provenance and appropriate license review.
- Superseded rules and unknown values cannot silently become active defaults.
- Public files are checked for secrets, private data and unintended local dependencies.
- Verified source transfer, offline behavior, live integration and business acceptance are reported separately.
